01
The Problem

AI has amnesia.
The cure became a cage.

Agents forget everything.
Close the tab and the context is gone. Every session starts from zero.
"Memory" products lock you in.
Mem0, Zep, ChatGPT memory — your context lives on their servers, in their schema, behind their model. Leave and it doesn't come with you.
You can't verify or revoke it.
No proof it wasn't tampered with. No real delete. "Privacy" is a settings toggle, not a guarantee.
"Context is becoming the moat. The models themselves are commoditizing — cost down 9–900× a year, the capability gap collapsing."
— the market consensus. The twist nobody's pricing in: if switching models is cheap, whoever owns the memory captures the durable value — not the model vendor.
